## Prompt utilizado

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a production 2D articulated sprite component atlas for OLHUDO, ONLY the navy rusty webcam monster at TOP RIGHT of the provided reference atlas. Reference image is identity/style reference. Preserve its navy cylindrical housing, orange rust speckles, concentric black/silver lens rim, one ivory bloodshot eye, small red recording LED on right side, and articulated desktop clamp. Same painterly cartoon dark outlines and three-quarter view facing LEFT. No redesign, no mouth, no teeth, no legs. Output square 1536x1536, exactly 3 columns x 3 rows of independent separated components, one component centered per invisible cell, at least 15% padding within each cell. Real transparent alpha background; no painted checkerboard, no labels, no text, no shadows on background. Components reading order: 1 TOP LEFT complete cylindrical webcam head housing alone, no base or cable, with EMPTY dark recessed lens socket instead of eyeball, intact concentric rim and red LED; 2 TOP CENTER complete ivory bloodshot eyeball oval matching socket perspective, painted veins and shading, NO pupil; 3 TOP RIGHT isolated black-purple oval pupil/iris with small white glint, matching eye perspective; 4 MIDDLE LEFT complete bottom desk clamp foot, including dark inner cavity and mounting socket, no head or upper arms; 5 MIDDLE CENTER a single short thick navy rusty support bar angled diagonally up-right, with COMPLETE round pivot hubs at BOTH ends, hidden surfaces fully painted; 6 MIDDLE RIGHT a second short thick navy rusty support bar nearly horizontal, COMPLETE round pivot hubs at BOTH ends, hidden surfaces fully painted; 7 BOTTOM LEFT a complete curved upper mechanical eyelid plate, navy metal with concentric silver/rust edge, shaped to cover upper half of the eye when lowered, with substantial concealed top overlap; 8 BOTTOM CENTER matching lower mechanical eyelid plate shaped to cover bottom half of eye when raised, substantial concealed bottom overlap; 9 BOTTOM RIGHT detached curved dark ribbed USB cable and its USB plug matching reference. All are finished individually painted reusable rigid parts, NOT an assembled character, not animation frames. Mechanical joint hubs must be full disks, not truncated ends. Background alpha zero.
